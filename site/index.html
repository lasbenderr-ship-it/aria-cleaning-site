Our Process

We follow the same three-step process for all our cleaning services to ensure you get the best possible results every time.

1

Understanding your current cleaning situation

  • In the first step, we try to get an understanding of the project/tasks at hand, including the tasks that need to be completed, whether you’re looking for long-term assistance or a one-off cleaning solution, and what your expectations are.
2

Site survey and Proposal

  • Next, we’ll perform an on-site survey to get an understanding of the project/tasks and the spaces that need to be cleaned. This helps us provide you with an accurate proposal. This proposal will be sent to you within one business day after completing the on-site survey.
3

Mobilisation

  • After agreeing on the project scope and terms and conditions, we can typically start work within two business days.
